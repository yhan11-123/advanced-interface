Read from the CSS this page runs on. Continuous, 0 to 100. The level is a number that rides on the needle, in the middle of the slider, always on screen; it rises and warms to lamp while the meter is held. The bars rise like the scale on an amp, and the speaker grows with the level, so the shape itself says louder to the right.
