Spec

Read from the CSS this page runs on. Continuous, 0 to 100. A ring of a hundred fine ticks wraps the whole capsule, one per percent: it starts at the bottom, climbs the right side, crosses the top and comes down the left. The ticks light in the lamp's colour up to the level, and a long marker stands at it. The level and a mood word read inside the lamp, always on screen, and the lamp shows it too, breathing a soft glow: warmer and dimmer when low, paler and brighter when high.
