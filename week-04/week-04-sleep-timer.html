Spec

Read from the CSS this page runs on. Continuous, 0 to 90 minutes, to the minute. The whole screen is the slider: night rises from the bottom, its starry dark climbing to the level, a warm horizon riding its edge; the top of the screen is 90 minutes. The minutes and the time the sound stops ride the horizon in big type, always on screen, above it, or below it once the night nears the top; a chip on the horizon's right end repeats the minutes.
