Spec

Read from the CSS this page runs on. One slider with three values: which of six layers, and where on it, across and along. In the box on the left the handle moves freely over a layer, pressing a dent into the mesh where it is, the way a knob moves over a colour wheel. Push it into a corner of the layer and it slips onto that edge of the box: a line you can ride up or down through the layers. Pull it inward and it steps onto the face of the layer you stopped at. On the right, the same layer seen from straight above, for fine work. The layer, the point and the force read in the corner, always on screen.

Box and layers

Field
BoxA wireframe box in isometric view, 1px white at 50%, its four upright edges the lines you ride
Layers
Layer in useDrawn at full strength; the others fade to 25%, the neighbours a little stronger
DentWhere the handle is, the mesh sinks up to 26px in a soft round dent, deepest while held
ColourEach strand coloured by how far it is pressed down, weak to strong

Handle and lines

Handle6px white dot with a dark rim, in a 10px ring; it sits on the mesh, in its dent
On a faceMoves freely: across and along the layer, to two decimals
Onto a lineWithin 0.18 of a corner, the handle slips onto that upright edge; the edge lights white with a tick at each layer
On a lineUp and down only, through the layers; it settles on the nearest one when let go
Back to a faceMove 16px inward off the line and the handle steps onto the face of that layer
From aboveThe panel on the right shows the layer in use flat; dragging there moves the handle at 0.7 speed, for fine work; a press there jumps it to that point. Both handles are the same point

Readout

PointMono 600 17px: across, along, from 0 to 1
ForceHow hard the mesh is pressed where the handle is, 0 to 100%
