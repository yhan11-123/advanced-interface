Spec

Read from the CSS this page runs on. One slider with three values: which of six layers, and where on it, across and along. The two halves split the work by direction. The stack on the left is for going between layers: press anywhere on it and the handle lifts onto a line through all six, and dragging up or down carries it from layer to layer until you let go. The flat view on the right is the layer you are on, seen from straight above: there the handle moves freely across and along it, the way a knob moves over a colour wheel, pressing a dent into the mesh. The layer, the point and the force read in the corner, always on screen.

Moving

Stack: between layersUp and down only. Pointing at the stack shows a line through the layers at the handle's point, the layer you point at labelled; press and the handle lifts onto it at that layer, drag up or down 28px a layer, let go and it settles on the nearest
Flat view: on a layerAcross and along only, at 0.7 speed for fine work; a press jumps the handle to that point
LinkedThe handle in the stack and the one in the flat view are the same point; each follows the other
