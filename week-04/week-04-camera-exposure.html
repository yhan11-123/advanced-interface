Spec

Read from the CSS this page runs on. Continuous, −2.0 to +2.0 EV. A half dial like a cockpit gauge, zero straight up: swing the needle left to darken, right to brighten, and the segments light from zero out to it. The value reads in the box under the hub, to a tenth, always on screen; the view above shows it too, with shutter speed and ISO following.

DragSwing round the hub, anywhere above it; lit segments turn amber, and the view, shutter and ISO follow with no lag
