Spec

Read from the CSS this page runs on. Stepped: five orbits, 400 km to 35,786 km. The slider is the orbits: the Earth in the corner, a ring for each orbit, and the spacecraft on a line running out from the planet. Drag it outward and it climbs ring to ring; the altitude reads big in the corner, always on screen, with that orbit's speed and period under it.
