ALT · KM

Spec

Read from the CSS this page runs on. Stepped: five orbits, 400 km to 35,786 km, on a vertical tape like an altimeter, low Earth orbit at the bottom and geostationary at the top. A readout box rides the tape and points at the orbit you are in, so the value is always on screen. The window shows it too: the Earth is drawn at the size it really looks from that height, a blue horizon filling the window down low, a small planet up high, and the orbit's speed and period read in its corners.
