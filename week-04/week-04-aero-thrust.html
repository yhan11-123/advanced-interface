Spec

Read from the CSS this page runs on. Continuous, 0 to 100% thrust, to the percent. The slider is the flame: grab the tip of the rocket's exhaust and pull it down to burn harder, push it back up to throttle off. The thrust reads beside the tip, always on screen; a scale runs down the right of the flame, and the stars rush past faster the longer it burns.
