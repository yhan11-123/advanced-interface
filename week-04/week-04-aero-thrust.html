Spec

Read from the CSS this page runs on. Continuous, 0 to 100% thrust, to the percent. The same throttle as before, seen from a different point of view: behind the ship, looking at the base of the booster. The throttle is a ring around its nine engines; turn it clockwise and the engines light one by one, the centre first and then round the outside, each the moment its share of the thrust is reached. The thrust reads big on the right, always on screen, under a six-second trace of how it has moved and over the count of engines lit.
