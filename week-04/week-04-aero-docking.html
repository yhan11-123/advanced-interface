Read from the CSS this page runs on. Stepped: five hold points on the way in, 200 m, 30 m, 10 m, 3 m and capture, drawn as gates in a tunnel the way an approach display draws it. Push up to fly forward through them, pull down to back off, or tap a gate to fly to it. The range reads big in the corner, always on screen, with the stage, closing rate and alignment round it; a ladder on the right shows which hold point you are at.
