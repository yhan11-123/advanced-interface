Spec

Read from the CSS this page runs on. Stepped, in tens: two sounds share one bar, split by a thin divider with a gap either side. Each part is that sound's share of the bar and carries the sound itself, its bars moving inside it, louder the bigger the share. Both shares read above the bar, always on screen, and add up to 100. Tap a sound's name to swap in another: rain, ocean, wind, forest or brown noise.
