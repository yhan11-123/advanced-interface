White noise mix

Spec

Read from the CSS this page runs on. Stepped, in tens: two sounds share one sound bar, and a divider sets how much of it each one gets. Left of the divider the bar takes the first sound's shape and colour, right of it the second's; the larger share also plays louder, so its bars stand taller. Both shares read below the bar, always on screen, and add up to 100. Tap a sound's name to swap in another: rain, ocean, wind, forest or brown noise.
