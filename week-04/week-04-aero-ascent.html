Spec

Read from the CSS this page runs on. Stepped, three tracks in one: a low orbit, a polar orbit and a transfer orbit round the Earth, sixteen fine marks along each. Grab any orbit and drag the vehicle along it; the globe turns as you go, each orbit turning it its own way about its own axis. Mission clock, velocity and altitude run across the top, always on screen; the orbit and the step read along the bottom.

States

HoverWithin 10px of a visible line, that orbit lights grey with its name and the cursor becomes a hand; the halo opens to 10px. Nothing moves yet. The part of an orbit behind the globe can't be picked, and the orbit in use wins where lines cross
PressedOn the vehicle, it is picked up where it is; on a line, that orbit becomes the one in use, the globe tilts to it and the vehicle glides to the point pressed. The halo tightens to 5px and the clock inverts. A press on empty space does nothing
DragThe vehicle follows the finger along its own orbit only, searching 1.5 steps either side of where it is, so it never jumps across the ellipse or to another track; the globe turns its way and ticks light as it passes
Let goGlides to the nearest tick
KeyboardLeft and Right one tick; Up and Down the next or last orbit; Home and End its first and last tick
