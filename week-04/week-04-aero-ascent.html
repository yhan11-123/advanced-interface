Read from the CSS this page runs on. Stepped: five events of one flight, liftoff to spacecraft separation, marked along the trajectory itself. Drag the vehicle along its path and the mission clock, velocity and altitude run with it across the top, always on screen; the event it sits on is named beside its marker. Let go and it settles on the nearest event; tap a marker to fly there.
