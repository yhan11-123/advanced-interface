A box is a span of time with a fossil record, named for its rock; its length is time. Inside it, lengths are shares of the finds, rough and for illustration. A break in a row is a gap in the record, not an absence of dinosaurs.

Spec

Read from the CSS this page runs on. Each region is a list item: leading, a ringed two-letter code; content, the name and the first record; the bar, its boxes in time; trailing, a chevron. Clicking a row opens it in place to show its rocks and, lane by lane, the families of each group; clicking a period goes one level down into it.
