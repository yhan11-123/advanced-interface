Read from the CSS this page runs on. Each place is a list item: leading, its country’s flag; content, the city, and under it the country and how many movements lived there; the bar, its blocks in time; trailing, a chevron. Every block is a button that goes one level deeper: the timeline slides away and a page for that movement in that place comes in, with what it grew from and where else it went (each a way across to that page), where it sits in the place’s row, its artists as list rows, and its works. “All places”, or Esc, comes back to where you were.
