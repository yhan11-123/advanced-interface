Inside the folder, the day's plans are the list, each row starting with its time and carrying its status as a tag, then one row build: leading, an airline or transport mark, a friend, a photo, or nothing; content, one line or two; trailing, a time, a price, a chevron, an arrow, a tick, or a value with an icon. The flight with a stop opens in place to show its legs; the hotel goes one level deeper, to its booking.
