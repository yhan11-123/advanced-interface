At rest each place shows only its main plate. Pointing at it slides its outer part out; only a click grows its middle part in and draws its construction lines. An exploded drawing laid on its side: one axis, a different plate for each place (a pyramid, a contour plate, a fan of planes, a dashed sheet, a contoured slab, a surveyed plot), sight lines at its head and an aquifer at its foot. Each plate has three parts, its pleats. Open on the first screen, compressed while you read; pointed at, it stretches open again.
