A place on the coil, and the same place in the list. At rest a circle on the line with its name and an arrow pointing in. Pointed at, the arrow thickens, the circle opens and the notes appear. Pressed, the circle closes small and the name leans in. Picked, the circle fills, the name is underlined, and the line is solid up to it and dashed beyond.
