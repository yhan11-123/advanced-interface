Every state

Collapsed, the board holds only the current print; pointed at, it expands and every cut-out and title comes out. Within it, the two parts of the navigation, the photos and the titles, at rest, pointed at, pressed and picked. A cut-out pointed at lifts and, moving, carries its afterimage; pressed, it sinks and the film stops; picked, it develops into a full print on an orange bar. A title at rest shows a stub of orange; pointed at (or reached along the path), the bar runs out under it; pressed, the bar thickens and the title dips; picked, the title is bold on a full, heavier bar.
