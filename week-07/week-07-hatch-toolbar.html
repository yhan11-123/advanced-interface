One button build in each configuration, at rest, pointed at and pressed: every button sits in a clean window slanted like the strokes, so its icon never sits on one; pointed at, the strokes gather round that window; pressed, the strokes inside it thicken from the press outward and close into one slanted block (here half way), with a band going out. Then the row (its first six tools) with nothing near, with the pointer resting, and with the pointer moving quickly; and the row against the left edge, in the middle and against the right edge.
