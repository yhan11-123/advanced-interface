Week 07 · Set 2 · ToolbarNine tools, each holding more. Press one and the toolbar folds: the tool keeps its place and takes a colour, its own tools open in a bar of the same colour just below, joined to it by an ink neck, and the rest of the toolbar comes down to join them by a thin bridge. Press another below and it folds again.

Press a tool to fold out the tools inside it.

Every state

One button build in each configuration, at rest, pointed at and pressed. A main tool that holds more carries a small dot. When its tools are out, the tool and its secondary bar share one pair colour; the neck between them stays ink (coral, apricot, mint, silver; each open pair a different one), so you can see which bar belongs to which tool; in the bar, icons are ink and the picked one sits on an ink disc. Then the folding itself: closed, one tool open, two open; and the toolbar against the left edge, in the middle and against the right edge.
