Week 08 · Set 2 · Week · ElectricA festival week drawn in light: Tidelight, seven days by the water. Pick a date and an orange frame slides to it, leaving a light trail; press it and the day switches on with its weather, stage chips, its list of sets and the pass. Chips filter the list by stage (any mix); the star saves a set to My lineup; open a set for its details and a link out; the split ticket buys a pass or opens a menu to choose one; the X closes the day, which derezzes. Every button is a chamfered line of light; pointed at, a streak races round it.
