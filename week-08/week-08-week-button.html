Week 08 · Set 2 · Week · CalmA festival week on a card: Tidelight, seven days by the water. Pick a date and the raised tile slides to it; press it and the day opens with its weather, stage chips, its list of sets and the pass. Chips filter the list by stage (any mix); the star saves a set to My lineup; open a set for its details and a link out; the split ticket buys a pass or opens a menu to choose one; dismiss folds the day away. Every button wears a thin metallic outline that catches the light.
