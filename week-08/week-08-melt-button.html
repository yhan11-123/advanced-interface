Week 08 · Set 1 · Melt · FluidA designer’s contact card. Your pointer carries a small cream drop, and every button is made of the same cream: Contact me fills halfway with cream that sloshes as you move, and, held down, fills to the top and pours out its options; Portfolio swallows it and its outline swells out toward your pointer; Résumé draws its outline round from where the drop touched; the pick runs from option to option. Press a button and its options drip out of it as drops that land as buttons on the row below; some open one row deeper.

Every state

Each level in each configuration, at rest, pointed at, pressed and turned off. Hover is drawn as it ends: Contact me full of cream (in use it fills halfway and fills to the top only while held), Portfolio with its bump, Résumé outlined in full. Turned off, the cream drains to a faint outline and nothing reaches for the pointer. Then the depth: each button closed, opened, and one level deeper.
