Week 08 · Set 3 · Make · SoftA button you make yourself. It starts as a rectangle of frosted glass with a blue light inside, soft as clay: push and pull it anywhere into the shape you want, type its label, then make it a button. Made, it answers like one: pointed at, the light rises and grows; held, it sinks; turned off, it goes out. Emphasis is how much light the glass holds.
