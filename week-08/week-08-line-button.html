Week 08 · Set 1 · Line · PreciseLineworks, a blueprint archive: an architecture office hands out the drawings of its buildings, and you pick a building, zoom in and download the sheet. A blueprint is a building drawn before it exists, so here a line shows what will happen before it happens: point at an option and its result is drawn on the sheet dashed, as a proposal; press, and it is drawn solid. Emphasis is the amount of line, as line weight is on the drawings: Blueprints has one faint line a pen draws round, Zoom has only crop marks that reach and close, Download, what you came for, has two lines that close into one heavy line. The tools stand in a column beside the sheet, in the order they are used, and each opens its options under itself; some open one level further.

Every state

Each level in each configuration, at rest, pointed at, pressed and turned off. Hover is drawn as it ends: Download’s two lines closed into one, Blueprints’ pen line drawn all the way round, Zoom’s crop marks closed. Pressed thickens or pulls in the line. Turned off, every line drops to a faint trace (Blueprints’ to a dotted one). Then the depth: each button closed, opened, and one level deeper.
