Week 08 · Set 1 · Line · PreciseA designer’s contact card drawn in outlines only, where emphasis is the amount of line. Contact me has two lines that close into one heavy line when pointed at; Portfolio has one faint line that a pen draws round bright; Résumé has only its two ends, like brackets, that reach along its edges and close. Nothing follows the pointer. Press a button and its options are drawn in just under it, each outline closing round before its words appear; some open one level further.

Every state

Each level in each configuration, at rest, pointed at, pressed and turned off. Hover is drawn as it ends: Contact me’s two lines closed into one, Portfolio’s pen line drawn all the way round, Résumé’s brackets closed. Pressed thickens or pulls in the line. Turned off, every line drops to a faint trace (Portfolio’s to a dotted one). Then the depth: each button closed, opened, and one level deeper.
